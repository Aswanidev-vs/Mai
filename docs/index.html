What she does

Everything a voice assistant should be.
Nothing it shouldn't.

Voice interaction that feels real

Streaming ASR, emotion-aware TTS, and lip-synced 3D character. Speak naturally — she adapts her tone to how you're feeling. Stressed? She slows down. Excited? She matches your energy.

Sherpa-ONNX Silero VAD Supertonic TTS

3D companion with a pulse

Real-time VRM character with breathing, eye saccades, micro-expressions, and mouse-tracking gaze. She blinks, fidgets, and reacts — not a static avatar, a living presence.

Zero data leaves your machine

No cloud. No telemetry. No API keys required. Your voice, your conversations, your data — all processed locally with ONNX Runtime. The only person listening is you.

She acts, not just answers

Function calling, app automation, UI control, web search, file management. Mai doesn't just tell you how — she does it. Open Chrome, send a WhatsApp, pull up that file.

Memory that actually works

Hierarchical memory: working buffer for context, episodic SQLite for history, semantic vectors for recall, procedural patterns for skills. She remembers what matters.

Anticipates, doesn't wait

Pattern learning from your habits. Time-of-day routines, frequent apps, conversation topics. After a while, she knows what you need before you ask.

Under the hood

From voice to action in under a second.

01

You speak

"Mai, open Chrome and search for Go concurrency patterns"

02

VAD detects speech

Silero VAD segments your voice from silence. Wake word "Mai" triggers the pipeline.

03

LLM reasons & plans

ReAct loop breaks it into steps: open Chrome, focus window, type query, press Enter.

04

Tools execute

RobotGo automation launches Chrome, types the query, presses Enter. Done.

05

Mai responds

"Done." — with emotion-adaptive TTS. Short, warm, done.
