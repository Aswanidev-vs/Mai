# Research Brief: Agent Swarm

## Refined question
What is the current state of agent swarm technology (multi-agent AI systems), what are the key frameworks and approaches, and what are the practical implications and open challenges?

## Scope boundaries
- **In**: Definitions, architectures, major frameworks (2024-2026), real-world applications, coordination patterns, open-source vs commercial approaches, current limitations
- **Out**: Single-agent systems, narrow chatbot implementations, theoretical swarm robotics (unless applied to LLM agents)

## Assumptions
- Audience: technically literate, wants both overview and practical insight
- Time frame: focus on 2024-2026 developments
- Include both academic and industry perspectives

## Depth mode
standard (3-5 sub-agents, 15+ sources)

## Date
2026-07-28

## Angles
1. **Core definitions & architecture patterns** — What is an agent swarm, how does it differ from single-agent or simple multi-agent systems, key coordination patterns (hierarchical, peer-to-peer, emergent)
2. **Major frameworks & tools** — CrewAI, AutoGen, LangGraph, Swarm (OpenAI), and other active frameworks as of 2025-2026
3. **Real-world applications & case studies** — Production deployments, notable use cases, companies using agent swarms
4. **Challenges, limitations & open problems** — Coordination failures, cost, reliability, hallucination propagation, evaluation difficulties
5. **Recent developments & future directions** — What's new in 2025-2026, emerging patterns, where the field is heading
